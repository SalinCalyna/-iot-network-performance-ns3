# Wi-Fi 6 risk study — figure captions

PDF (vector) and PNG (300 dpi) in `figures/`. Generated by `experiments/wifi6-risk/make_report_outputs.py`.

## F-W1_pdr_vs_k
Nominal PDR versus the number of high-risk relays k (8 kbps per sensor). Left: N = 30, seeds 20–29 (n = 10); right: N = 100, seeds 20–22 (n = 3). Markers: mean; error bars: Student-t 95 % interval of the mean (descriptive only for N = 100); faint dots: individual seeds. Nominal PDR = delivered / scheduled packets. The N = 30 Static/Risk-Aware k = 0 seed at 96.7 % is seed 28 (one source isolated in the 90 m disk graph). AODV at N = 100 is affected by the route-maintenance behaviour of F-W5–F-W7 at every k.

*Source:* seeds10-n30-8k-v2, n100-8k-v2 analysis/pilot_runs.csv

## F-W2_exposure_vs_k
Exposure rate (share of sent packets whose path crossed at least one high-risk relay) versus k; layout and intervals as F-W1. High-risk relays are selected from Static's BFS tree (subtree rule), which maximises Static's exposure by design. Risk-Aware exposure is 0 except N = 30 seed 21 (source 11 has no safe path: 3.33 % in that seed at k = 2–3, mean over 10 seeds 0.33 %).

*Source:* as F-W1

## F-W3_paired_differences
Paired Risk-Aware − Static differences on the same seed (same positions, traffic and high-risk set) for nominal PDR (top) and exposure (bottom). Grey: individual seeds; orange: mean with Student-t 95 % interval. N = 30 (n = 10): all 10 pairs favour Risk-Aware at every k = 1–3; exact Wilcoxon p = 0.00195, Holm-adjusted 0.0117 over the six primary tests. N = 100 (n = 3): descriptive only, no test.

*Source:* paired_riskaware_vs_static.csv (both batches)

## F-W4_cost_delay_hops
Cost of risk avoidance. Columns: delay p50 and p95 over delivered packets (mean ± Student-t 95 % interval of per-run percentiles) and mean path length in links (seed means). Solid: delivered packets; dashed, open markers: all sent packets (path planned/traversed). Static's falling delay and hops with k are survivorship: packets routed through risky relays are lost. At N = 100 Risk-Aware's all-sent path length equals Static's (1.613 links at every k, curves overlap), so its delay is unchanged from k = 0. At N = 30 Risk-Aware does lengthen paths (1.64 → 1.75 links at k = 3), and its p95 delay rises by about 2 ms over k = 0.

*Source:* pilot_runs.csv; runs/*/packet_paths.csv

## F-W5_aodv_olsr_pdr_time
AODV and OLSR at N = 100, 8 kbps, k = 0: PDR of packets sent in each 30 s window, seeds 20–22. AODV delivers 39.7–65.8 % in every window, from the first window onward; OLSR delivers 98.9–100 %.

*Source:* n100-8k-v2 runs/{aodv,olsr}_k0_*/packet_paths.csv

## F-W6_aodv_link_failures_phase
AODV diagnostic, N = 100, 8 kbps, k = 0, seed 20 only. Left: AODV link-failure events (SendRerrWhenBreaksLinkToNextHop) per second in 10 s bins, with the Block Ack modification on (original run, byte-identical rerun) and off. Failures already occur before traffic (Hello expiry) and rise about 2.5× within 10 s of traffic start, then stay flat; the fix makes no visible difference. Right: phase of RREQ originations and link failures within the 512 ms synchronized CBR cycle (fix on); 39.9 % of RREQ originations fall in the first 32 ms. Counts are observations; the feedback loop that may link them is a hypothesis (T-W6c).

*Source:* aodv-diag-n100-20261024T204129/*/runs/*/aodv_log_counts.json

## F-W7_frame_composition
Transmitted PSDUs by frame type at N = 100, 8 kbps, k = 0 (log scale; bars: mean of seeds 20–22; dots: seeds). AODV sends 9.9–11.4× OLSR's broadcast frames, 2.8–3.2× its unicast data PSDUs and 129–164× its BlockAckRequests. Broadcast includes routing control (AODV RREQ/RERR/Hello; OLSR HELLO/TC).

*Source:* n100-8k-v2 runs/*/wifi_txvector_counts.csv

## F-W8_delay_cdf_n100
Cumulative distribution of end-to-end delay of delivered packets, N = 100, 8 kbps, k = 0, seeds 20–22 pooled (packet counts in legend). Static and Risk-Aware coincide at k = 0. Delivered packets only: AODV's curve excludes its 43 % lost packets. The knee in AODV's curve near 500 ms coincides with the MAC queue lifetime (500 ms); this is an observation, not a tested cause.

*Source:* n100-8k-v2 runs/*_k0_*/packet_paths.csv
