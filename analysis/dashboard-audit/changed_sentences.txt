Throughput, Delay, Packet Loss, Routing Overhead, and Path Changes have no predefined thresholds in the research methodology, so each is classified relative to its own median value across all measured baseline conditions (AODV/OLSR/Static × N=10–100) — at or worse than the median is flagged High Risk.
V4/Sigmoid is not offered as a Protocol option here because its datasets don't yet have the full per-protocol metric set (Throughput, Delay, Packet Loss, Routing Overhead, Path Changes) needed for consistent risk classification, unlike AODV/OLSR/Static.
